Nuvio Arc is my experimental fork of Nuvio for trying new ideas, not a replacement for the official app. It runs on the same boxes and works best on stronger ones. It's a one-person hobby project: report problems or ideas in this repo's Issues (best effort) or at r/NuvioForks, and please don't report issues from this build to the official Nuvio GitHub or Discord.

A hotfix on top of **Nuvio Arc 1.1.0-nt.8**, still built on **official NuvioTV 1.1.0-beta.8**. It installs over nt.8 and keeps all your settings.

Night mode on nt.8 turned effects and bass down along with the loud scenes, so films sounded flat and quiet. It now only lifts the quiet moments. This release also brings frame rate matching back in line with the official app for 25 and 30 fps video.

---

# Fixed

- **Night mode lifts quiet moments instead of turning loud ones down.** On nt.8 it cut the bass even on Light and drowned out effects on Strong, and typical dialogue got quieter too. Now quiet scenes and soft speech come up (by up to 6 dB on Light, 10 dB on Medium and 15 dB on Strong), while loud effects and bass keep the level they were mixed at. A limiter still catches the peaks, and a sudden loud hit right after a quiet, lifted scene is caught sooner. The same change applies with ExoPlayer and MPV. (#69)
- **25 and 30 fps video switches the TV to 25 and 30 Hz again.** Since nt.8, frame rate matching sent 25 fps to 50 Hz and 30 fps to 60 Hz even when the TV offers 25 or 30 Hz. It now picks the exact rate when the TV has it, like the official app. Motion looks the same either way, since at 50 Hz each frame was just shown twice. 50 or 60 Hz is still used when there's no exact mode (720p usually has no 25 Hz), and on TVs that switch refresh rate without going black (QMS), where a switch to 25 Hz would black out the screen.

---

# Good to know

- **Night mode makes everything louder,** most of all on Strong, because it now lifts the quiet parts and leaves the loud ones as they were. Turn the TV down a little when you switch it on. As before, it never changes sound passed straight through to a soundbar, receiver or TV.

---

# Install

The update banner in the app installs this release for you. Or download the APK for your device below and install it over the top (your data is kept):

- **`app-full-arm64-v8a-release.apk`** for most current boxes (Shield, Ugoos, Google TV).
- **`app-full-armeabi-v7a-release.apk`** for the Fire TV Stick and other 32-bit devices.

Nuvio Arc installs over earlier Nuvio Arc and Nuvio-test builds, keeps all your settings, profiles and sign-ins, and still installs next to the official Nuvio. Version **1.1.0-nt.8.1**, package **`com.nuvio.tv.test`**.
